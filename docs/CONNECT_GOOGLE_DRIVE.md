# Connect your Google Drive to Trippy

Trippy will save trip receipt photos in **your** Google Drive. This takes about 2 minutes.
You only do it once.

**What Trippy can see:** only the receipt files Trippy itself puts in your Drive.
It **cannot** see, open or delete any of your other files, emails or photos.

---

## Steps

1. **Open the link** you were sent. It looks like this:
   `https://fady-trip-tracker.vercel.app/api/google/connect`

2. **Choose your account.** Google asks you to pick an account.
   Pick the account with the big storage. If it isn't in the list, click
   **"Use another account"** and sign in to it.

3. **If you see "Google hasn't verified this app"** (you may not see this at all):
   click **Advanced**, then click **Go to Trippy**.

4. **Allow access.** Google shows that Trippy wants to
   *"See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app"*.
   Click **Continue** (or **Allow**).

5. **You'll see a page saying "✅ Google Drive connected".**
   - Check that the **Account** shown is the right one (the big storage account).
   - Press the **Copy** button.

6. **Send the code you copied** to the person who sent you the link,
   in a **private message**. Don't post it in a group chat.

That's it. 🎉

---

## After connecting — please don't

- ❌ Delete the folder named **"Trippy Receipts"** that will appear in your Drive. It holds all the receipts.
- ❌ Remove Trippy from your Google account
  (Google Account → Security → *Your connections to third-party apps*). That would stop receipt uploads.

## Something went wrong?

- **"This link expired"** → open the original link again and repeat the steps.
  Finish them in one go, within 10 minutes.
- **You picked the wrong account** → just open the link again and choose the right one,
  then send the new code instead.
